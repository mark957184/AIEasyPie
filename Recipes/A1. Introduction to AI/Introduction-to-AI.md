## How AI was born?
Chill, I'm not gonna talk for the entire documentation about history (to be honest it's boring for me too, as it is for almost everybody), but to trully understand how it works and why today we have **this** kind of AI and not another one I (unfortunately) have to talk about it. How it all started? It hasn't started directly with AI, but it all started with computers. No, I'm not talking about computers like those expensive 64 GB RAM, with Nvidia RTX 5080, 2 TB of storage, OLED 4K 165Hz gaming PCs we have today, we're talking about wired potatoes! 

Those slow and big as hell machines were first used for science, then to automatize calculations, but the majority of it's evolution happened during the second world war. They absolutely speeded up the research about automatized calculations and decryption (sending messages to your allies that were far away from you without it being understood by the enemy was crucial in a war without mass media).

After the war, some scientists started to realize that the real world doesn't always have a linear solution that you can solve with basic math formulas. For example, how should a machine recognize a number? just think about it, If we feed it a simple gray-scale image, the machine just sees a grid of numbers representing how dark each pixel is. There is no single mathematical equation in the universe that can look at that grid and say "Yep, that's a 7". But wait, we can recognize perfectly a number, even in a low resolution and gray-scale image...IDEA! Why don't we make machines think like us? 

That's how the first AI systems were born, using the same logic of a human brain.


## So, what is AI?
For this one I want to be precise. So, as some dictionaries says, AI (Artificial Intelligence) is "the capability of a computational system to perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence, this encompasses processes like learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding and..." blah blah blah. 

What does it mean? It means, as we said, that AI is the capability of a server, for example, of doing things that a potato (compared to a server) like your computer (yea, yours. Even if you have a gaming monster) can't do. Things like recognizing an image as one of an animal rather than one of a human. Is that impressive?
